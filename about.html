About Us

Photo by Joe Blyth

Snow Trails Agency began with Founder Lucas Snow's simple passion for exploring the beauty of winter. Founded alongside his group of friends who loved skiing, snowy landscapes, and mountain adventures, we wanted to help others experience the magic of the snow. What started as weekend trips to the mountains grew into a travel agency dedicated to creating unforgettable winter holidays. Today, Snow Trails Agency helps travellers discover amazing snowy destinations, from peaceful mountain escapes to exciting ski adventures, while making every journey easy, enjoyable, and memorable.
