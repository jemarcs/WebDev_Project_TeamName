Top Alpine Ski & Snow Resorts

  • Verbier, Switzerland: Ranked number one for its serious alpine terrain, vibrant après-ski scene, and high-altitude runs at 3,330 meters.
  • Val Thorens, France: The highest resort in Europe, sitting at an altitude of 3,230 meters with guaranteed snow and extensive skiing.
  • Silvretta Arena Ischgl-Samnaun, Austria/Switzerland: Features a massive network of 239 km of connected runs and a high-energy winter atmosphere.
  • Breuil-Cervinia, Italy: Located at 2,050 meters altitude with scenic views right near the Matterhorn.
  • Goderdzi, Georgia: An up-and-coming Eastern European mountain pick offering 13 km of pistes and a 690-meter vertical drop.
